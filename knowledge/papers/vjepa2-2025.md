# V-JEPA 2: Self-Supervised Video Models Enable Understanding, Prediction and Planning (Assran, Bardes et al., 2025)

**Lab:** Meta FAIR · **Code:** https://github.com/facebookresearch/vjepa2 · **Read state:** abstract-only

## One-paragraph summary

1B+-parameter video JEPA pre-trained action-free on >1M hours of internet video; strong
motion understanding (77.3 top-1 SSv2) and SOTA action anticipation (Epic-Kitchens-100).
The headline for us: **V-JEPA 2-AC**, an action-conditioned world model post-trained on
only ~62h of *unlabeled* robot video, deployed zero-shot on Franka arms for
pick-and-place planning. Pre-train passively at scale, then specialize the world model
with a sliver of interaction data.

## Relevance to our experiments

The pre-train-passive → post-train-action-conditioned recipe is the scaled version of
what our ladder builds toward; the planning-with-latent-MPC approach is the alternative
to [[imagination-training]] we should eventually compare.
